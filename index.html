Tutorials

1. Import and arrange clips

Open the Editor, click Import, then double-click a media item or drag it onto a track. Drag clips to move them, and drag the right edge to trim.

2. Split, color and animate

Press S to split at the playhead. Select a clip and use Effect Controls for color grading, speed, green screen and start/end keyframes for scale and position.

3. Titles, transitions and export

Click Add Title for captions. Choose Fade, Wipe or Slide in Transition. Press Export to render a WebM file in real time.

Plugin Center

Choose a plugin, customize it, preview it, then click “Add to Timeline” to use it in your current video project. Works locally in your browser.

🎛 Plugin Controls

Quickly find a tool, jump to its controls, or open your video editor.

Plugin workflow

  1. Choose a plugin below.
  2. Adjust its controls and preview the result.
  3. Click its Add to Timeline button.
  4. Open the Editor Workspace to move or adjust the new clip.

Some tools, such as color look previews and the export checklist, are helpers and do not create timeline clips.

🎬 Editor Workspace

Open your editing workspace, import media, arrange clips, preview your video and export.

Workspace workflow

  1. Open the Editor Workspace.
  2. Import your video, images and audio into Media.
  3. Drag or add clips to the timeline and arrange them.
  4. Use Plugin Center to add supported text, captions, stickers and audio.
  5. Return to the workspace to review and export your project.

Your project timeline is the main place to arrange and review clips. Some plugin tools are previews or helper utilities rather than timeline effects.

❔ Quick Help

How do I use a plugin?

Open Browse Plugins, select a tool, customize its settings, preview it, then use its action button. Text and sticker plugins require an open project before adding to the timeline.

How do I add audio?

Choose an audio file in Audio Waveform, wait for it to load, then click Add Audio to Timeline.

Why can't I record voiceover?

Allow microphone access. Some browsers require the app to run on HTTPS or localhost.
